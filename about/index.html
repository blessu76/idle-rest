About idle()

소개

// rest was written into the code

idle() is the music of ipe(), a one-person studio. It is lo-fi and healing music for tired developers, and for anyone whose mind keeps running after the day is done. Put it on when you pause between tasks, when you need to focus late at night, or when you simply want to rest.

What kind of music

  • Lo-fi for resting. Tracks sorted by the hours of the mind: night, focus, rest, dawn.
  • English and Korean. The same songs in two languages; the lyrics are written and refined by a person.
  • The same goodbye every time. Every track ends with one whispered line: "ipe. be still".
  • You can listen on YouTube, including the first album, dear(you); vol.1.

Made with AI, openly

The music of idle() is made with the help of AI music tools, and we do not hide it. A person writes the lyrics, chooses the tracks, refines them and shapes the album. We also mark AI use on YouTube.

About this site

Music does not play on the site itself; pressing a track takes you to YouTube. To keep the site running, Google AdSense ads may be shown. Details on ads and cookies are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
