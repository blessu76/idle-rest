Questions

// short answers

Is idle() good lofi for coding?

Yes if you like slow, soft music beside you. The songs are unhurried lo-fi and ambient pieces, and they do carry a quiet voice and lyrics, in English and in Korean. Pick the version that stays out of your way.

Is the music made with AI?

Yes, with Suno. Lyrics are AI-drafted and reviewed by a person. The journal explains the steps, from lyric to release.

Where can I listen?

Every song is on YouTube at @ipe-rest, in English and Korean versions. The site links you straight to each track.

What should I play for a long night of coding?

Start with the single still compiling, then the album dear(you); vol.1 from track 01.
