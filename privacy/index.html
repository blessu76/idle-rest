02Tools the Site uses

  1. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — usage statistics such as visits, page use and track-link clicks. It uses cookies (_ga, _ga_*). idle.ipe.rest and the home site ipe.rest share the same GA4 property. Provider: Google LLC (including affiliates such as Google Ireland Limited).
  2. Cloudflare Web Analytics — counts page views and performance metrics without cookies. Provider: Cloudflare, Inc.
  3. Google AdSense — serves ads on the Site. See Section 5.
  4. GitHub Pages — stores and serves the Site's files. GitHub, Inc. may process server access logs (including IP addresses) for security and operations.
  5. Browser storage — your choices (language ipe_lang, display theme idle-theme) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the Operator's server and you can clear them in your browser. A service worker also keeps page and icon files on your device so the Site can open offline.

Music does not play on the Site itself. When you press a track button, a short redirect page sends you to YouTube. What happens to your data after you reach YouTube is governed by Google's and YouTube's policies.

03Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. On this Site, Google Analytics and Google's advertising services use cookies. You can allow, block or delete cookies in your browser settings (for example Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies; Safari: Settings > Privacy). The Site works with cookies blocked, though your language and theme choices may not be remembered. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

04Disclosure to third parties and processors

The Operator does not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties for payment, and does not disclose it without your consent except where the law requires. The service providers listed in Section 6 (including Google, which serves the ads) communicate directly with your browser and process the automatically collected information in Section 1.

05Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Site displays ads through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this Site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can visit www.aboutads.info to learn how to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.
  • For how Google uses data when you use partner sites, see How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Behavioural information (online personalised ads): the items are website visit history, ad click records and ad cookie identifiers; they are collected automatically through cookies; the purpose is to serve personalised ads and measure performance. Google collects and processes this information, and the Operator does not receive it in a form that identifies individual visitors. The Site does not target ads by inferred sensitive categories (such as health or political views) and does not run personalised ads aimed at children under 14.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: consent for personalised ads and cookies is collected through Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP), the "Privacy & messaging" feature in AdSense. You can decline or change your choice in that message at any time. If you do not consent, you may see non-personalised ads.
  • Users in regions that give a right to opt out of ad personalisation, such as some US states, can also choose through Google Ads Settings and the AdSense message.

Turning personalised ads off does not remove ads; you may still see general, context-based ads.

08Your rights and how to use them

You may ask the Operator at any time to give you access to, correct or delete your personal information, to stop processing it, or to withdraw your consent. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will handle it without delay and tell you the result. A representative may make the request on your behalf. You can refuse cookies and personalised ads yourself as described in Sections 3 and 5. 09Children under 14
