When a song speaks to someone at the end of a day, we tend to imagine a place: a window, a cup, a lamp. How much of that place is actually written down? We looked in the English lyric sheets (Version A) of dear(you); vol.1 for the everyday objects and weather a listener could picture, and counted them. The music is made with AI (Suno) and the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## The method first

We read the same 2,554 English words of the ten design documents we used in earlier counts, written on 2026-07-04 when the songs were commissioned, so what is sung may differ. We picked six setting words before counting: "window", "coffee", "rain", "lights", "room" and "phone". We also looked for "tea", "lamp", "moon", "star", "door" and "blanket" and the singular "light", and none of them appears in the sheets. The list is ours and short, so it leaves out other objects we did not think of. Words for time, such as "night" (6) and "morning" (3), are a different kind of word and are not in the total.

## 19 words, mostly in one song

The six words appear 19 times in 2,554, under 1% of the words.

| Word | Times | Songs |
|---|---|---|
| window | 6 | 01, 05, 09, 10 |
| coffee | 4 | 01, 05, 06, 07 |
| lights | 3 | 05, 06, 10 |
| room | 3 | 02, 06, 10 |
| rain | 2 | 05, 09 |
| phone | 1 | 05 |

By song, song 05, "It's Okay to Rest a While.", holds 7 of the 19: window three times, and coffee, rain, lights and phone once each. Song 10 has three (window, lights, room) and so does song 06 (coffee, lights, room). Song 01 has two (window, coffee), song 09 has two (window, rain), songs 02 and 07 have one each, and songs 03, 04 and 08 have none.

Not every "room" is a cosy one. In songs 02 and 06 it is a meeting room that the speaker walks out of, and only in song 10 is it "here in the room". The lights in song 06 are the ones "under the same lights today", not a lamp at home.

## Against the heart and tomorrow

Next to these, two other words appear much more. "Heart" is in the sheets 13 times across eight songs, and "tomorrow" 20 times across nine, which we followed in [an earlier post](/journal/en/counting-tomorrow-and-a-little/). The sheets say more about what is felt and what comes next than about what the room contains. Song 05 is the one that sets the scene most, which fits its theme in the design document: rest, with a look out the window and a cup of coffee in the story.

## What this does and does not say

We cannot say how a listener pictures the place, and a design document is not a recording. A small word count of objects does not mean the songs lack atmosphere, since the music, the voice and the cover carry some of it, and our list of words is only ours. What the sheets show is limited: the setting is sketched with a few objects, most of them in one song, and the rest of the lines are about the listener.
