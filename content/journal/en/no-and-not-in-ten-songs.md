Songs written to comfort someone often say what does not have to happen. Do not hurry, do not answer, do not try harder. We wanted to know how much of dear(you); vol.1 is built from that kind of sentence, so we counted the negative words in the English lyric sheets (Version A) of all ten songs. The music is made with AI (Suno) and the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## The method first

We used the same 2,554 words of English lyrics that we counted in [an earlier post on you, I and we](/journal/en/you-i-we-in-the-english-lyrics/), read from the ten design documents written on 2026-07-04. They are documents from the day the songs were commissioned, so what is sung may differ. A negative word here is "no", "not", "never", "nothing", "none" or any contraction ending in "n't", such as "don't", "wasn't" or "didn't". Words like "without" and "hardly" are not counted, and neither is "nobody", which does not appear.

## 50 in 2,554

There are 50 negative words in 2,554, about 2.0%, or one in every 51 words. The spread by song is wide.

| Song | Words | Negative words | Per 100 words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 232 | 7 | 3.0 |
| 02 | 206 | 5 | 2.4 |
| 03 | 296 | 6 | 2.0 |
| 04 | 306 | 7 | 2.3 |
| 05 | 267 | 5 | 1.9 |
| 06 | 263 | 4 | 1.5 |
| 07 | 250 | 2 | 0.8 |
| 08 | 250 | 6 | 2.4 |
| 09 | 243 | 4 | 1.6 |
| 10 | 241 | 4 | 1.7 |

Song 01, "Hard Day Again, Wasn't It?", is the most negative by rate at 3.0 per 100 words, and song 04 has the same count of seven in a longer sheet. Song 07, "I'm Glad You're Here.", is the least negative: two words in 250, which is 0.8 per 100.

## Which words they are

"Don't" is the most common with 13. Then come "no" with 7, "never" and "not" with 6 each, "wasn't", "didn't" and "nothing" with 4 each, "doesn't" with 3, and "none", "haven't" and "isn't" once each.

Eleven of the 13 "don't"s belong to one phrase, "you don't have to": three times in song 01 and twice each in songs 08 and 10, with one each in songs 02, 03, 05 and 07. That is a phrase in seven of the ten songs. The other two "don't"s are in song 04 ("don't always last the longest") and song 03 ("don't exist to keep up with the speed"). "For no reason" appears three times, in songs 03, 04 and 08. "Nothing at all" appears four times, in songs 04, 05 and 10, and twice in song 05. Song 09 uses "never" three times, which is three of its four negative words.

## What this does and does not say

Counting words does not tell us what a sentence means. "Nothing at all" can be a worry or a relief, and a line like "it's not enough yet" in song 08 is a negative word used for a feeling the song then answers. We read the sheets as documents, not recordings, and we have not checked whether the same share holds in the sung versions. The count shows only that the most repeated way of saying a negative in these ten songs is permission: "you don't have to".
