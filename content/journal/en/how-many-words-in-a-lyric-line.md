A lyric sheet is written in short lines, but how short? We counted the words in every line of the English lyric sheets of dear(you); vol.1 (Version A). The music is made with AI (Suno) and the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## The method first

We read the English lyric section of the ten song design documents, written on 2026-07-04 when the songs were commissioned, so what is sung may differ. We skipped section markers, whispered lines and the closing signature, which leaves the same 673 lines and 2,554 words we used in [the post on one-word lines](/journal/en/one-word-lines-in-ten-songs/). A word is a run of letters, and an apostrophe stays inside it, so "wasn't" is one word. A line here is a line of the sheet, which may differ from a sung phrase.

## Most lines have two to five words

| Words in the line | Lines | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 79 | 11.7% |
| 2 | 130 | 19.3% |
| 3 | 98 | 14.6% |
| 4 | 131 | 19.5% |
| 5 | 100 | 14.9% |
| 6 | 76 | 11.3% |
| 7 | 37 | 5.5% |
| 8 | 16 | 2.4% |
| 9 | 6 | 0.9% |
| **All lines** | **673** | **100%** |

The average is 3.79 words and the middle line has 4. Four-word lines are the most common, 131, with two-word lines right behind at 130. Lines of two to five words add up to 459, which is 68.2% of the sheets. At the long end, 59 lines (8.8%) have seven words or more, and no line has ten. The six nine-word lines sit in songs 01, 02, 03 (two), 04 and 05; one of them is "Your coffee had gone cold somewhere along the way,".

Song 01, "Hard Day Again, Wasn't It?", has the longest lines on average, 4.30 words, followed by song 05 at 4.24. Song 08, "You Were Enough Today.", has the shortest, 3.38, and song 06, "You're Not Alone.", is next at 3.46. These are the same two songs that have the most one-word lines in [the earlier count](/journal/en/one-word-lines-in-ten-songs/), so their short lines are not a few stray words but an average that sits lower.

## Where in a song the lines get short

| Section | Lines | Words per line |
|---|---|---|
| Verse 2 | 114 | 4.32 |
| Verse 1 | 121 | 4.17 |
| Chorus | 99 | 4.01 |
| Pre-Chorus | 72 | 3.74 |
| Bridge | 114 | 3.47 |
| Final Chorus | 82 | 3.45 |
| Outro | 71 | 2.99 |

The two Verses hold the longest lines and the Outro the shortest, 2.99 words, the one section under three. The section lengths themselves are in [the post on which section runs longest](/journal/en/which-section-runs-longest/).

## Limits

The lines follow the design documents, where a line break may be a writing habit and not an instruction to the singer, and a recorded version may join or split lines differently. Counting words is also not the same as counting syllables, so a four-word line can be sung faster or slower than another. We counted only English lines of Version A, not whispers, and not the Korean lines (see [how Korean lyric lines end](/journal/en/how-korean-lyric-lines-end/)). What the sheets show is a habit of short lines, mostly two to five words, that gets shorter in the Final Chorus and the Outro.
