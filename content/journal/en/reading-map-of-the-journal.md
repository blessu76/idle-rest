Before this one, this journal holds twenty-four entries, written between 22 October and 4 November 2026, and the list on the journal page runs newest first. That order is good for following along, but not for finding your way in. This entry is a map: those twenty-four, grouped by the question each one asks. It adds no new facts about the music; it only points. The music is made with AI (Suno); the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## If you want to know how the music is made

- [How an AI Lo-Fi Song Gets Made](/journal/en/how-a-song-gets-made/) is the shortest way in: who hands what to whom, and where we check.
- [The Six Series of idle()](/journal/en/six-series-of-idle/) explains the series names and song codes that look like code.
- [From Korean Draft C to the First Single](/journal/en/from-draft-c-to-good-morning-world/) and [still compiling — One Night, Two Languages](/journal/en/still-compiling-one-night-two-languages/) each lay a lyric draft beside the lyrics that were finally sung.
- [82 Draft Lines, 15 Cues](/journal/en/how-much-of-the-draft-reaches-the-screen/) counts what a short clip keeps of a long draft.

## If you want to see the album as a whole

*dear(you); vol.1* is ten songs, and five entries read all ten at once, each by a different measure:

- [The Ten Steps of Feeling](/journal/en/ten-steps-of-feeling/): the album plan, from empathy to peace.
- [How One Lyric Skeleton Repeats Across Ten Songs](/journal/en/ten-letters-one-skeleton/): the shared structure of the lyric sheets.
- [Ten Titles, Read as Sentences](/journal/en/ten-titles-as-sentences/): tense, speaker and tone of the titles.
- [Who Speaks in the Ten Titles?](/journal/en/who-speaks-in-ten-titles/): a count of the pronouns.
- [The Tempo Ladder of Ten Songs](/journal/en/tempo-ladder-of-ten-songs/): the written BPM, song by song.

If you read only one of these, the first gives the frame the other four count inside.

## If you want to know why every song has two versions

Each song exists in an English-main version and a Korean-main version. [English by a Woman, Korean by a Man](/journal/en/english-female-korean-male/) gives the rule that splits them, and [Whispers in the Other Language](/journal/en/whispers-in-the-other-language/) counts the whispered lines that switch language between the two.

## If you want to know what comes next

[One Day, Four Albums](/journal/en/one-day-in-four-albums/) lays out the four-album plan for Season 1. Only the first album is out, and that entry says which parts of the plan are still open.

## And the site itself

[One Entry, Twelve Languages](/journal/en/one-entry-twelve-languages/) explains how an entry becomes up to twelve pages. A related detail from the list itself: twenty-one of the twenty-four entries exist in all twelve languages, and the three newest, the tempo ladder, the pronoun count and the language entry, are published in Korean and English only so far.

## What this map does not say

- It does not rank the entries. Grouping is our reading, not a measure of which entries are read most; we have not measured that.
- It does not promise an order. Each entry stands alone, and any of them is a fair place to begin.
- It does not cover what is not written yet.

If you have read one entry and want the next, follow the links inside it; most point to a neighbour.
