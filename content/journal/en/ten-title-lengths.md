The song titles of this album are sentences. How long are they? This post counts the Korean and English titles of all ten songs in syllables, words and letters.

The scope and method first. We read the title (KR) and title (EN) in the metadata table of each song design document. Korean syllables are the number of Hangul characters (가-힣). English words are the number of space-separated words. English letters are the number of alphabet letters, with spaces and punctuation left out. The design documents were written on 2026-07-04 when the songs were commissioned, and the YouTube titles after release may differ. The music is made with AI (Suno); the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

The totals are 78 Korean syllables and 44 English words. Per song that is 7.8 syllables and 4.4 words on average.

## Reading the lengths

In Korean, two songs have 6 syllables (03, 06), three have 7 (05, 08, 09) and five have 9 (01, 02, 04, 07, 10). No song has 8.

In English, two titles have 3 words (04, 06), three have 4 (03, 07, 08), four have 5 (01, 02, 09, 10) and one has 6 (05).

The shortest titles are song 03, "나도 무서웠어." (6 Korean syllables), and song 06, "You're Not Alone." (3 English words). By English letters, songs 03 and 06 are tied at 13, the shortest, and the longest is song 09, "Let's Smile a Little Tomorrow.", at 24.

Words and letters tell different stories. Song 04 has only 3 English words but 17 letters, while song 05 has the most words, 6, and 19 letters, the same as songs 01 and 02.

One more small thing: the word 오늘도 ("today, too") is in the Korean titles of three songs, 01, 08 and 10.

## What we are not claiming

- That the title lengths were chosen on purpose. The design documents do not say so.
- That the post-release YouTube titles match this table. We counted the titles in the design documents and did not check YouTube.
- That a shorter title is easier to remember, or anything about how a listener will feel. No medical or other effect is claimed.
