Every song design document for dear(you); vol.1 has a "Mood" section with four English words. Ten songs make forty slots. This post counts all forty and checks how many different words they hold.

The scope and method first. We read the "Mood" line in each of the ten design documents. Those documents were written on 2026-07-04 when the songs were commissioned, so the recorded and mastered songs may differ from them. A mood here is a style intention that was given to Suno, the tool that makes the music. It is not a measurement of how the songs sound. The music is made with AI (Suno); the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## The ten moods

| Song | Four mood words |
|---|---|
| 01 | Warm · Gentle · Quiet · Comforting |
| 02 | Safe · Warm · Emotional · Gentle |
| 03 | Reflective · Gentle · Emotional · Hopeful |
| 04 | Warm · Hopeful · Gentle · Peaceful |
| 05 | Peaceful · Warm · Calm · Cozy |
| 06 | Warm · Hopeful · Gentle · Emotional |
| 07 | Warm · Hopeful · Gentle · Bright |
| 08 | Gentle · Warm · Peaceful · Hopeful |
| 09 | Hopeful · Warm · Peaceful · Gentle |
| 10 | Peaceful · Gentle · Warm · Intimate |

## Counting them

Four words per song, ten songs: forty slots. The number of different words is thirteen.

| Word | Songs it appears in |
|---|---|
| Warm | 9 |
| Gentle | 9 |
| Hopeful | 6 |
| Peaceful | 5 |
| Emotional | 3 |
| Quiet · Comforting · Safe · Reflective · Calm · Cozy · Bright · Intimate | 1 each |

The top four words (Warm, Gentle, Hopeful, Peaceful) fill twenty-nine of the forty slots. Eight words appear only once.

Only one song lacks Warm: song 03. Only one song lacks Gentle: song 05. Song 03 is also the only one with Reflective. Song 05 has Calm and Cozy, and neither appears anywhere else.

## What we checked, and what we could not

For songs 02 to 10 we confirmed that the mood in the design document matches the mood in the song's package document (package.md), in all nine cases. The package document for song 01 has no mood line in the same format, so we could not compare that one.

## What we are not claiming

- That the repeated words were chosen on purpose. The design documents do not say so.
- That these moods can be heard in the songs. We counted words and did not analyse the audio.
- Anything about how a listener will feel. No medical or other effect is claimed.
- Anything about the mood in song 01's package document, which we could not check.
