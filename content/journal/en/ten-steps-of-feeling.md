The ten songs of *dear(you); vol.1* are tied together in the album plan as one order of feeling: ten steps that start at empathy and end at peace. [How a Song Gets Made](/journal/en/how-a-song-gets-made/) noted in one paragraph that this order is drawn before any song is written. This post reads the ten steps one song at a time. For each song it asks what the song does at its place and how it connects to the one before, and it writes down only what the design documents and lyric drafts support. Where a single song already has its own post, we link to it.

First, the scope. We read the album plan and the song design documents (the lyric drafts and the notes). Anything said about tempo or sound is a value written in those documents, not an impression from listening. What is actually sung on a recording can be shorter than the draft. And as always: the music is produced with AI (Suno). Lyrics are AI-drafted and reviewed by a person.

## The order was written down first

The concept section of the album plan begins by saying the album forces no advice, no solutions and no resolutions on anyone (our translation). In the arc summary under its track list, the plan then gives the road the ten songs travel in one line (our translation of the words):

> empathy → understanding → anxiety → reassurance → rest → connection → gratitude → affirmation → hope → peace

The plan adds that the ten songs as a whole flow like one long letter that draws "a tired day's night → rest → the next morning" (our translation). The track list table has, for every song, a Key Emotion, a BPM, and a short note in a column headed 결 (roughly "feel"). Two emotions are set in bold in that table: rest for song 05 and peace for song 10.

Put the tempo next to the words and one shape shows. Song 04 is highest at 74, and the two bold cells hold the two lowest lower bounds of the ten (song 05 at 66–70, song 10 at 64). Song 05 is a range that overlaps song 08's 68, so this compares the lower end. The same shape is also read in [Putting Rest at the Center](/journal/en/rest-at-the-center/). Whether that pairing was intended is not recorded.

## One line per song

### Song 01 · Empathy — a check-in before any fix

The theme field of the design document says this song offers no solution and gives no advice (our translation). The first line of the song is a question: "It was hard again today, wasn't it?" Verse 1 closes this way.

> Today, let me be the first to ask how you've been.

The first word of the ten steps is empathy, and the song reads as one that asks before it comforts. The post that reads this song on its own is [Check In Before You Fix](/journal/en/check-in-before-you-fix/).

### Song 02 · Understanding — listening without demanding an answer

The note for song 02 marks it as the place where the previous song's empathy is "deepened into understanding" (our translation). If song 01 asked first, song 02 waits for the answer. The chorus runs:

> I will listen to the very end. You don't have to answer at all.

The one who asks becomes the one who listens. Empathy and understanding sit side by side at the front, and what the two songs do can be read as a pair.

### Song 03 · Anxiety — touching the uncomfortable place for the first time

The theme field says the song does not try to remove the fear and acknowledges the feeling first (our translation). The album plan calls this attitude the emotional center of the album and singles out songs 03, 04 and 07. After two songs that asked and listened, song 03 is the first to speak the anxiety of the AI era directly.

> What if I'm slowly fading away?

The story field says the song does not answer the question "Will I be okay from here on?" (our translation). How it is built is read line by line in [Admitting Fear Instead of Fixing It](/journal/en/i-was-scared-too/).

### Song 04 · Reassurance — an answer to song 03

The design note for song 04 calls it the "answer song" to the previous song, the one about anxiety (our translation). Reassurance follows anxiety directly, and the note explains that place as a reply to what the previous song said. Its title is "You're Still Shining." The one sentence its Bridge shares with song 07 is read in [The Same Sentence in Two Songs](/journal/en/same-sentence-in-two-songs/).

### Song 05 · Rest — setting the speed down in the middle

The note describes this song as the "center of the emotional arc = rest (the quietest track, Sleep Lo-fi)" (our translation). A sentence in its Pre-Chorus says how the song works.

> Just as a song is beautiful only because of its rests, the time you take to rest is part of your day too.

What stands out is that rest is written as a step of the arc, not a pause outside it. The post on this song alone is [Putting Rest at the Center](/journal/en/rest-at-the-center/).

### Song 06 · Connection — back to the people beside you

The note for song 06 says that after catching its breath in rest, the album "turns its gaze back to the people beside you" (our translation). The lyric starts from a day of smiling as if fine and moves toward discovering the people who were there all along, the ones who left a review and handed over a coffee.

> I thought I was holding on alone, but there were people quietly walking beside me.

### Song 07 · Gratitude — not the best, the one who stayed

According to the theme field this song is a letter to the one who stays beside you, not to the one who is good at things. The note says it follows from the thanks for the "together" found in song 06 (our translation). The lyric names its measure like this.

> the one I think of isn't the fast one, but the warm one.

### Song 08 · Affirmation — the gaze turns to yourself

The note for song 08 says that where gratitude faced others, this song "turns its gaze to yourself" (our translation). The theme field starts from the habit of remembering what we failed at before what we did well (our translation). The song moves by words that let that habit go.

> You don't have to prove anything more.

### Song 09 · Hope — a hope that isn't grand

The story field keeps hope small: it says hope needs nothing grand, only one heart willing to start the day again (our translation). The Bridge says something close.

> Hope was never far away — it was only the heart that rises again and opens the window.

The instrument field lists Morning Birds (Very Subtle), and the note describes that as foreshadowing the move from night to morning. The album plan also says that with this song the night begins to lift.

### Song 10 · Peace — no advice, no encouragement

The theme field of the last song says there is no advice, no encouragement and no resolve (our translation). It is the song written at 64 BPM, the slowest of the ten. The lyric divides the next day like this.

> Leave today's worries with today, and let's meet tomorrow's hope tomorrow.

The pair that opens the album with a question and closes it with comfort is read separately in [Open with a Question, Close with Comfort](/journal/en/open-with-a-question-close-with-comfort/).

## Notes that pick up the previous song

Read the ten design notes in order and one thing shows. In six of the ten songs, songs 02, 04, 06, 07, 08 and 09, the note calls the previous song's feeling by name: deepened, answered, back to people, thanks that follow, the gaze turned to yourself, past self-acceptance and on to tomorrow.

![Ten circles joined in a row. Between 1 and 2, 3 and 4, 5 and 6, 6 and 7, 7 and 8, and 8 and 9 there are gold arrows with labels: deepened from empathy to understanding, answer song to song 3, back to people after rest, thanks for togetherness, from others to yourself, past self-acceptance toward tomorrow. Between 2 and 3, 4 and 5, and 9 and 10 the line is dotted](/assets/journal/ten-steps-of-feeling/memo-chain.jpg)

*Where a note names the song before it (gold) and where it does not (dotted). The gold text is the gist of each note.*

That does not mean the other songs are disconnected. The notes for songs 03, 05 and 10 name only their own feeling, and song 01 is the starting point, so it has nothing before it. The picture counts the words the notes use. There is no written rule that says notes should be written this way.

## The words connect too

The album plan's list of recurring devices includes a shared vocabulary: it's okay, slowly, enough, thank you, rest well. The plan says these words fix the comforting tone across the whole album (our translation). We counted fragments of these five words in the Korean-main lyric drafts.

![A table of ten songs by five words. The stem for "it's okay" appears in all ten songs, 3 to 6 times each; "slowly" in nine songs, all except song 4; "enough" in eight, all except songs 6 and 7; "thank you" in eight, all except songs 5 and 9; "rest well" in three songs, 1, 5 and 8. The totals are 49, 20, 17, 9 and 3](/assets/journal/ten-steps-of-feeling/shared-words.jpg)

*Counts of word fragments in the Korean-main lyrics, whisper lines excluded. The first column counts the stem of "it's okay" in its several forms.*

Only one stem appears in all ten songs, the one for "it's okay". It runs six times in songs 1 to 3 and in song 6, and three times in song 10 as the lowest count, but it never drops out. "Enough" is missing from songs 6 and 7, "thank you" from songs 5 and 9, and "rest well" appears only in songs 1, 5 and 8. The vocabulary does not cover the ten songs evenly. It comes and goes from song to song. Why a given word is absent is not recorded. The counts on the whisper side are in [Whispers in the Other Language](/journal/en/whispers-in-the-other-language/).

## Sound, as written in the design documents

The sound section of the album plan says the album moves "night → morning" (our translation) and splits that move into stretches. Songs 01 and 02 get rain and café ambience. Songs 03 and 04 are Emotional Chill with gentle strings coming in. Song 05 is the quietest Sleep Lo-fi. Songs 06 to 08 carry human warmth (keyboard sounds, the same light) and a warm acoustic sound. Song 09 is Dream Pop Ambient with Morning Birds, very faint. Song 10 is the slowest, Ambient Piano. That the album plan splits the order into six stretches (songs 01–02, 03–04, 05, 06–08, 09 and 10) that move in one direction, from night toward morning, can be checked in the documents. We did not check whether the recordings sound that way.

## What is not recorded

- Why the arc has ten steps, and why in this order. The album plan states the order and not the reason.
- When the ten words were fixed. The Key Emotion fields in the design documents mostly match the album plan's table, but for song 04 the plan's table says "comfort → reassurance" while the song's own document says "comfort → reassurance → hope" (our translation). Which one is the reference is not written down.
- Whether the order reaches a listener the way it reads on paper. We read documents and drafts and did not listen to the recordings. The songs were released one at a time as singles, so a person who hears only one song may not see this order.
