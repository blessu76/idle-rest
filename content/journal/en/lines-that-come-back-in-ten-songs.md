A song is partly made of things that return. A chorus comes back, a phrase is said twice, a word is set down again at the end. This time we counted the simplest kind of return in the lyric sheets of dear(you); vol.1: a line that appears again in the same song, word for word. The music is made with AI (Suno) and the lyrics are drafted by AI and reviewed by a person.

## The method first

We read the English lyric section (Version A) of the ten song design documents, written on 2026-07-04 when the songs were commissioned, so what is sung may differ. We skipped section markers, whispered lines and the closing signature, which leaves 673 lines, the same set we used in earlier counts of these sheets. To compare lines we lowercased them and removed punctuation. A line "comes back" only if it is identical after that, inside one song. Repeats across songs are not counted, and neither are lines that only resemble each other.

## 630 distinct, 43 extra

Of the 673 lines, 630 are distinct. The other 43 are extra copies of a line already sung once, 6.4% of all lines. Most of the sheets are written out in full, with little exact repetition.

Songs 01 and 02 repeat exactly one line each: "It was hard again today, wasn't it?" in song 01 and "Take your time and tell me" in song 02. Song 06, "You're Not Alone.", has ten extra repeats, the most. Five of those ten come from the single word "someone", which stands alone as a line six times in the sheet. The song also repeats "today too" four times (three extra), "today" once and "You're not alone." once.

## Short lines do most of the returning

Of the 43 extra repeats, 13 are one-word lines and 20 are two-word lines, so 33 are one or two words long. The longest repeated line has seven words, "It was hard again today, wasn't it?" in song 01. Two more have six words ("Take your time and tell me" in song 02 and "It's okay to rest a while" in song 05), and two have five ("I'm so glad you're here" in song 07 and "Would it be so bad" in song 09). A returning line here is usually a small one.

The single biggest returner is "today too". It appears 19 times across five songs: twice in song 03, four times each in songs 04, 06 and 07, and five times in song 08. It links to the [word "today"](/journal/en/the-word-today-in-ten-songs/) we followed earlier, and the line is short enough to be a refrain by itself.

## What this does and does not say

An exact-match count leaves out the many near-repeats, such as a chorus rewritten with one word changed. It also reads a design document, not a recording, and a repeated line on paper can be sung differently each time. We do not know whether the returns were planned as refrains or simply fell out of the drafting. What the sheets show is narrow: the lines that return are small, and a few songs return more than the rest.
