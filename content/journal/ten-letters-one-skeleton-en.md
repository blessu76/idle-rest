*dear(you); vol.1* is idle()'s first album, ten songs long. Open the lyric drafts one after another and the same shape keeps coming back. This post walks through that skeleton by track number, going by the drafts. What is actually sung on the recordings can be shorter than the draft.

One thing up front: the music is produced with AI (Suno). Lyrics are AI-drafted and reviewed by a person. We would rather say so from the first line.

## Six slots, and a seventh

Every lyric follows the same order: Verse 1, Verse 2, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Outro. Tracks 1 (S1A1.01) and 2 (S1A1.02) stop at those six.

From track 3 through track 10, a Final Chorus sits between the Bridge and the Outro, making seven. Why that change was made isn't written down anywhere. What we can confirm is that on track 4, "You're Still Shining.", the Final Chorus doesn't just repeat the chorus. It is rewritten with new lines, and it ends on this one:

> Today, too, you're still shining.

## English and Korean, one blueprint

Each song is made in two versions: one with English as the main language (female vocal), one with Korean as the main language (male vocal). Both versions share the same sections, and their line counts differ by 0 to 1 per section. One version runs 56 to 82 lines, counting the whispers and the closing signature (titles and blank lines excluded). The English side is adapted, not translated word for word, because literal translation breaks the rhythm and the feeling.

A small device ties the two versions together: the whisper. The English version carries a Korean word or two, and the Korean version carries an English one. In the English version of track 1, a whispered *괜찮아…* ("it's okay") appears inside the first verse.

## The last line of every song

Every Outro, in both languages, ends on the same line: a one-breath whisper, fixed in English, "ipe. be still". It is the sound version of the brand line `ipe(); // be still`, meant to work like a logo you hear.
