Who runs this site

idle.ipe.rest is operated by {{OPERATOR}}, an individual developer. ipe() is short for “idle process” — the state a computer enters when it has no work to do and simply rests. It is a small, independent studio making quiet, developer-minded things, and idle() is its music.

You can reach the operator at {{EMAIL}} (see Contact).

How the music is made

We do not hide how the music is made. The music is produced with AI (Suno). Lyrics are AI-drafted and reviewed by a person. Since the dear(you); album, every track closes with a quiet spoken signature — “ipe. be still”.

Our first album, dear(you); vol.1, is ten letters to the you who made it through another hard day. Two singles, “good morning, world” and “still compiling”, sit beside it.
