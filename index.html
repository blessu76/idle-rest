How idle() is made

We make a quiet gap: music for the moment your work pauses.

idle() is the music of ipe(), a one-person studio. The sound is made with the help of AI music tools, and we say so. A person writes the lyrics, chooses the takes and shapes each album.

  • Written by a person

    Lyrics, concept and message are written and refined by hand.

  • AI, disclosed

    We mark AI use on the site and on YouTube. Nothing is hidden.

  • One soft goodbye

    Every track ends with a whispered “ipe. be still”.
