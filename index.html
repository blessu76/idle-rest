About

ipe()

idle() is the music of ipe() — a small AI-native studio. The name is a computing term: the state a processor enters when it finally has nothing left to do, and simply rests. Music is made with AI, openly; the words are written by hand. There's a stillness underneath it all — be still, and know — carried gently, never preached.
